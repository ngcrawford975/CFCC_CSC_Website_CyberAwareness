Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-Factor Authentication is a way of adding extra layers of security to your accounts. In most cases, it requires two different ways of you identifying yourself to gain access. Some examples of this are obvious, such as entering a password and then receiving a text on your phone. One of the earliest examples, and one we still do everyday, is needing not only your card to make a purchase, but also needing to enter a PIN. Studies show that utilizing multi-factor authentication can significantly reduce the risk of unauthorized access to your accounts, blocking around 99% of unauthorized access attempts. This is why it is so important to enable MFA when given the option. The few extra seconds of typing in a code sent to your phone, can save you from losing access to to your personal information, financial assets, and overall online security.
